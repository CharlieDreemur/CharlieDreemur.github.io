I am happy to finish the game [Bomb Card Showdown](https://charliedreemur.itch.io/holobombcard) in a 2-week hololive game jam.

## Description

Ready for a one-on-one "Bomb Card Showdown" with your Hololive idol? It was supposed to be just a friendly game with Gura, but something feels… off. A shadow lurks beneath her usual smile, twisting her expressions as you begin each turn. This is no ordinary game—it's a fight against the darkness creeping into her soul.

## What's my role in the game

As the only programmer and the lead in the team, I have:

- Based on **Unity 6 URP**, implemented PBR Toon Shader, Toon Bloom, Visual Sky, SDF Shadow, PerObjectShadow to achieve **anime-style rendering** effects.
- Implemented card game and AI mechanics using an **event-driven architecture**, leveraging **command** and **strategy design patterns** to support complex AI behaviors and card interactions.
- Integrated character animations, including morph targets and **inverse kinematics** with card animations to synchronize gameplay and character responses.
- Designed an optimized workflow for importing MMD and FBX models, applying **anime-style rendering**, and incorporating these assets into the game with high fidelity.
