ZombieBlockade is a tower-defense game where players need to build various walls and towers, keep a balance between economic and defense power, and protect themselves against waves of zombies.

Role: Lead Programmer

- Construct a grid-based building system enabling players to construct walls and towers for strategic defense against zombies
- Implement a Navmesh pathfinding system adaptable to dynamic grid-based obstacles, enhancing AI navigation
